## Thesis in one paragraph

Centrus is the only US-owned enricher and the only Western producer of HALEU (high-assay
low-enriched uranium), the fuel most advanced reactors need. Q2 2026 revenue was $176.1M vs $154.5M
a year earlier; FY2026 guidance was raised to $450-500M; it holds a $900M DOE HALEU enrichment award,
a $3.0B contingent LEU+HALEU backlog, HALEU supply contracts with Oklo (up to five microreactors,
deliveries from 2029) and Antares, and it raised $500M for the Piketon expansion [41][42][43][44][29].
Uranium term prices are at a nominal record (~$96.50/lb) with Kazatomprom cutting 2026 output and
facing a Russian sulphuric-acid export ban (nuclear-energy-power.md [49][51]). The 10x case is that the
market comes to price Centrus as the Western HALEU monopolist with multi-GW of advanced-reactor fuel
demand contracted, plus large-scale LEU capacity replacing Russian supply under the 2028 statutory
import ban [from memory, verify].

## What 10x implies

- Implied market cap: ~$30B, ~60x 2026 revenue guidance.
- Precedent: Cameco was worth more than that in 2025 [from memory, verify], so the size has a
  sector precedent; the multiple does not. A $30B Centrus needs revenue above ~$2B by 2028 at a
  15x sales multiple, or a strategic premium (a national-security acquirer) that the market has not
  paid for an enricher.
- The honest read of the same numbers: a **2-3x** (to $6-9B, ~12-18x sales on a $500M base) is a
  30 % proposition; a 10x is a 3 % proposition.

## Base / bull / bear (subjective)

- **Bull (P ~3 %):** multi-GW HALEU contracts, Piketon on time, strategic premium: 10x.
- **Upside-base (P ~30 %):** backlog converts, guidance beats, sector momentum returns: 2-3x.
- **Base (P ~40 %):** capex absorbs cash, stock tracks the uranium cycle: 0.7-1.5x.
- **Bear (P ~27 %):** DOE award cut or delayed, cost overrun, advanced-reactor timelines slip
  (as they did in 2026): -40 to -60 %. Note the limited downside relative to the other candidates:
  Centrus has revenue, contracts and a government customer.

## Kill criteria

1. DOE award reduced or cancelled.
2. Enrichment expansion cost overrun >50 %.
3. Backlog conversion stalls for two consecutive quarters.

## What the sceptics say

Capital intensity; the stock fell on good news after the $500M raise [43][44]; Urenco and Orano are
expanding Western capacity [NOT FOUND this session]; advanced-reactor demand for HALEU depends on
SMR developers whose own timelines slipped in 2026 (Oklo -44 % YTD, NuScale no binding PPAs;
nuclear-energy-power.md).

## Position-sizing logic

Centrus is not a 10x bet; it is the highest-quality name in the nuclear screen with a 2-3x path and
a bounded downside. For the 10x sleeve it is a diversifier rather than a core holding. IV is likely
60-90 % [from memory, verify], so LEAPS are unattractive; stock only.

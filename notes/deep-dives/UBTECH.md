# Deep dive: UBTech Robotics (9880.HK)

**Date:** 2026-09-24  **Price at writing:** HK$78.20 (2026-09-24 close, Yahoo)  **Market cap:** HK$39.4B
(~US$5.0B; Yahoo, 2026-09-24). The screen's figure was HK$80.10 / HK$46.5B on 21 Sep [56]. 24-month
high HK$157.70 on 2025-10-02; the stock is at half that. **Evidence grade:** medium (single result
set, one date; interim-report figures as reported by news).

## Thesis in one paragraph

UBTech is the only sizeable listed humanoid-robot pure play outside China's A-share market. H1 2026
revenue doubled to RMB 1.27B and the full-size humanoid segment grew 1,445 % to RMB 590M, while the
stock fell ~40 % year-to-date [57][58]. Volume inflecting while price falls is the shape of the
pre-10x set-ups in `00-base-rates.md` (earnings inflection plus prior drawdown). If humanoid revenue
compounds from RMB 590M per half to several RMB 10B per year with positive gross margin, and Tesla
Optimus or Figure slip so that UBTech is the volume leader, the market could re-rate it as the
listed humanoid leader.

## What 10x implies

- Implied market cap: HK$465B (~US$60B).
- Precedent: robotics companies have been worth that and more (Intuitive Surgical well above
  $100B; Fanuc ~$30B [both from memory, verify]). Goldman's "second half of the AI era" framing [59]
  is the narrative that would carry it.
- Required revenue: at a generous 10x sales, ~US$6B (RMB ~43B) per year, i.e. ~17x H1 2026
  annualised. At 5x sales, ~RMB 85B. Both need a multi-year ramp that has to be visible by 2027.

## Base / bull / bear (subjective)

- **Bull (P ~5 %):** humanoid revenue >RMB 10B in 2027 with positive gross margin; re-rating to
  US$40-60B.
- **Base (P ~50 %):** growth continues but margins are negative; placements at discounts; 1-2x.
- **Bear (P ~45 %):** unit definition includes education/toy humanoids (see below), margins stay
  negative, HK small-cap liquidity dries up: -50 to -80 %.

## Kill criteria

1. Humanoid segment revenue growth < +50 % YoY in any half.
2. Negative humanoid gross margin for two consecutive halves.
3. A placing at a discount >15 %.
4. Evidence that the "16,123 full-size units" [57] includes education/toy models. **This must be
   checked in the H1 2026 interim report before any entry**: RMB 590M / 16,123 implies ~RMB 37k
   per unit, implausibly low for industrial humanoids.

## What the sceptics say

Governance and disclosure standards of HK-listed Chinese tech; a history of losses [from memory,
verify]; humanoid unit economics unproven anywhere; the A-share supply chain (Leader Harmonic,
Estun) may capture more value than the integrator; the stock already fell 40 % into accelerating
growth, which is either an opportunity or the market's verdict on margins.

## Position-sizing logic

P(10x) ~5 %, P(loss >50 %) ~45 %: EV per unit ~ +0.13 (loss 0.65); Kelly ~2 %. Small position,
entered only after the unit-definition check. No liquid options market; stock only.

## 1. Why this theme could produce a 10x in 1-2 years

**Quantum computing.** The structural change since the last 10x wave (Sep 2024 - early 2025) is
that the sector has moved from "four micro-caps with a narrative" to a funded, multi-listing
industry with real, if small, revenue:

- Capital flow. Quantinuum raised $1.68B in the first traditional IPO of a full-stack quantum
  company (priced $60, 28M shares, 4 Jun 2026, implied $14-15B) [28][29][30]. Infleqtion listed
  on NYSE via SPAC on 17 Feb 2026 with >$550M gross proceeds including a $125M PIPE [24][25].
  Horizon Quantum listed on Nasdaq via SPAC on 20 Mar 2026 with ~$120M gross proceeds [33][34].
  SEEQC filed an S-1 on 29-30 Jun 2026 for a Nasdaq listing as SEQC (Cantor/BTIG) [39]. IonQ
  held $2.12B of cash and short-term investments at 30 Jun 2026 [4][5]; Rigetti $541.3M [12][13];
  D-Wave $546.2M [15][16]; QUBT $1.3B [18]. The sector is not going to run out of money in the
  24-month window; the question is whether it can spend it into something the market will pay for.
- Revenue is now measurable. IonQ Q2 2026 revenue $80.1M (+287% YoY), FY2026 guide raised to
  $280-290M [6][7]. D-Wave H1 2026 bookings $35.5M (+1,120% YoY) with RPO $40.7M [15][16].
  QUBT went from $61k to $5.6M quarterly revenue in a year, largely via the NHanced
  Semiconductors acquisition ("Fab 2") [18][19].
- Technical milestones with dates in the window. IBM's roadmap puts Kookaburra (first module
  combining qLDPC quantum memory with a logical processing unit) in 2026, Cockatoo in 2027,
  Starling (~200 logical qubits, 100M operations) in 2029 [43][44][45]. Google Willow (Dec 2024)
  demonstrated below-threshold error correction on superconducting qubits [45]. Rigetti's
  Cepheus-1-108Q reports ~99.1% median two-qubit fidelity across 12 interconnected 9-qubit
  chiplets, with a ~1,000-qubit / 99.9% two-qubit target over ~3 years [12][13].
- Government money. Rigetti has a letter of intent with the US Department of Commerce for up to
  $100M of CHIPS Act funding [12][13].

**Robotics/humanoids.** The one hard datum found: UBTech's H1 2026 revenue doubled to RMB 1.27B
and its full-size humanoid segment revenue rose 1,445% to RMB 590M, while the stock is down ~40%
year-to-date [57][58]. That combination (volume inflecting, price falling) is the shape of a
pre-10x set-up *if* the revenue is real and margin-bearing. Goldman Sachs has publicly argued
the "second half of the AI era" shifts focus from chips to humanoids, and raised Leader
Harmonic's expected share of high-spec harmonic reducers to 30% (Harmonic Drive Systems 70%)
for 2025-2030 [59].

**Space / defence.** Not researched in this pass. The a-priori catalyst clusters that need
checking: a SpaceX/Starlink IPO in the window and which listed names would trade as proxies;
European rearmament budgets; US drone procurement. See section 5 to-do list.

## 2. What the market already prices

**Quantum.** The late-2024 10x wave is fully in the price and then some. Current market caps
(Sep 2026): IonQ ~$16.5B [1][2][3], Quantinuum $13.53B [31][32], D-Wave $6.26B [14],
Rigetti ~$5.1-5.7B [9][10][11], Infleqtion $3.17B [26][27], QUBT $1.91B [20], Horizon
Quantum ~$850M [35][36]. Against that, sector revenue run-rate is roughly $300M+ (almost all
IonQ) and every company guides to large losses (IonQ adjusted EBITDA loss guide -$310M to
-$330M for 2026 [49][47]). Sentiment has already had one reset: from late June to late July
2026 IonQ fell 39%, Rigetti 30%, D-Wave 30% [46][47]; further rate-driven drops in mid-August
[48]. Quantinuum, the sector's best-capitalised name, trades at $53.02 (22 Sep 2026) versus a
$60 IPO price and a $86.79 post-IPO high [31][32]. Bank of America at Quantum.Tech (3 Jul 2026)
said the industry still lacks "commercially relevant algorithms and fault-tolerant hardware"
before broad quantum advantage can emerge [47].

Dilution is the mechanism by which the last run was monetised: IonQ's diluted share count rose
71% between Sep 2024 and Jun 2026 [4]; shares outstanding 381.0M at 30 Jun 2026 vs 362.6M at
31 Dec 2025 [5][8]. Arqit has an F-3 shelf on file [63]. Any new 10x in these names would be
fighting a much larger base and a management habit of selling into strength.

**Sceptic timeline, for the record.** Jensen Huang, Jan 2025: useful quantum computers 15-30
years away (stocks fell double digits); Mar 2025: "I was wrong", said he had not realised
there were listed quantum companies [41][64]; Jun 2025: "inflection point"; Oct 2025: NVQLink
launched; by Mar 2026 NVIDIA's quantum story is "a maintenance story" [41]. Scott Aaronson's
contemporaneous view on the Jan 2025 crash is at [42]. Net: the loudest sceptic reversed, but
the physics timeline (IBM fault tolerance 2029) has not moved.

**Robotics.** UBTech is priced at HK$46.5B (~US$6B at the HKD peg) [56] after a 40% YTD fall
[57]. Serve Robotics ($377M) has just cut 2026 revenue guidance to $9-10M from $26M [51][52].
Richtech ($438M) has ~$5M flat revenue and -$20.6M TTM losses [53][54]. Symbotic at $26.6B
is not a 10x candidate on size [55].

Detail on the two names that survive a first look:

**UBTech (9880.HK).** What has to be true for 10x: humanoid revenue compounds from RMB 590M/half
to several RMB 10B/yr with positive gross margin, and the market re-rates it as the listed
humanoid leader outside Tesla. Catalysts: FY2026 results (Mar 2027); H1 2027 results (Aug 2027);
further auto-plant contracts; any Tesla Optimus / Figure production slip that leaves UBTech as
the volume leader. Kill criteria: humanoid segment revenue growth below +50% YoY in any half;
gross margin on humanoids negative for two consecutive halves; a placing at a discount >15%;
evidence the unit count includes toy/education humanoids. Evidence quality medium; confidence
that the price/market-cap data are correct: medium (single result set, one date).

**QUBT.** What has to be true for 10x: the NHanced fab becomes a credible merchant photonics
foundry with $100M+ revenue, plus a quantum product the market believes; cash provides a floor
that limits the loss side. Catalysts: Q3 2026 results (Nov 2026); fab utilisation disclosures.
Kill criteria: cash falls below $1.0B without a matching revenue step; any going-concern or
restatement language; another large ATM at a discount. Evidence quality high on financials
(8-K) but low on the quantum technology itself. Probability of 10x: low; included because it
is the only listed quantum name where cash covers most of the market cap.

## 4. Rejected candidates and why

Quantum:
- **IonQ (IONQ)** – $40.74, mkt cap ~$16.5B (23 Sep 2026) [1][2]. 10x = ~$165B, ~570x the 2026
  revenue guide. Best revenue in sector but 71% dilution since Sep 2024 [4] and a $1.65B
  non-cash warrant charge in Q2 [8]. Bellwether, not a 10x.
- **Quantinuum (QNT)** – $53.02, $13.53B (22 Sep 2026) [31][32]. 10x = $135B. Trades below the
  $60 IPO price; Honeywell/Cambridge Quantum hold ~82% [28], an overhang. Note the
  "+604% since IPO" market-cap statistic in [31] is a float artefact, not a return.
- **D-Wave (QBTS)** – $6.26B (24 Sep 2026) [14]. 10x = $63B. Q2 revenue $3.1M flat YoY; Q2
  bookings fell to $2.1M after $33.4M in Q1 [15][16]. Annealing roadmap (20k qubits 2029) does
  not address gate-model advantage; Quantum Circuits acquisition is an admission of that.
- **Rigetti (RGTI)** – $16.53, ~$5.1-5.7B (22 Sep 2026) [9][10][11]. 10x = ~$55B on ~$20M
  annualised revenue [12]. Real technical progress (Cepheus-1-108Q) but three years from its
  own 1,000-qubit target.
- **Infleqtion (INFQ)** – $14.90, $3.17B (22-23 Sep 2026) [26][27]. 10x = $32B. Revenue
  [NOT FOUND]. Neutral-atom + sensing is a differentiated story, but at $3B it is priced as a
  top-4 name already; 52-week range $8.52-27.50 shows how much air is in it.
- **Horizon Quantum (HQ)** – ~$15.40, ~$850M (19 Sep 2026) [35]. Pre-revenue software;
  management says monetisation only when quantum advantage arrives [35]. 10x = $8.5B for a
  pre-revenue IDE. The "+2,442% YoY" market-cap figure [36] is a SPAC-shell artefact.
